What is FitPlan?

FitPlan is a free fitness plan generator that creates personalised nutrition and workout plans based on your unique body metrics, goals, and lifestyle. No generic templates. No cookie-cutter programs.

Every plan is calculated using proven formulas — Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, evidence-based macro splits, and progressive overload principles — to give you a plan that actually works.

How It Works

  1. Fill in your details — name, age, weight, height, goal, and preferences
  2. Click Generate — your personalised plan is created instantly
  3. Follow the plan — daily meal schedules, workout splits, and progress tracking built in
