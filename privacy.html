Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is how we handle your data on FitPlan.

What Data We Collect

When you use FitPlan, the following information may be collected through the forms on our website:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Physical attributes (age, weight, height, body measurements)
  • Health and fitness data (gender, fitness goals, dietary preferences, health conditions)

Why We Collect It

We collect this data solely to generate personalised fitness and nutrition plans tailored to your body type, goals, and lifestyle. The information you provide directly influences the plan calculations and recommendations.

How Your Data Is Used

All calculations and plan generation happen locally in your browser. Your data is not sent to any server unless you choose to purchase a premium plan. For premium plans, selected details are sent via Telegram to our team so your customised plan can be created and delivered to you.

Data Storage

We do not store your data on any server or database. All fitness plan calculations are processed entirely in your browser. Premium plan data is transmitted via Telegram and is not retained in any centralised system.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Telegram is used solely as a communication channel for delivering premium plans and responding to contact form messages.

Contact

For any privacy-related concerns or requests, please reach out via our Contact page or email us directly at sky270197@gmail.com.
